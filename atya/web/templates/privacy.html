Privacy policy

Effective 13 November 2026

ATYA is an invite-only personal memory assistant. You send it messages on Telegram, WhatsApp or its web page; it files them into notes, tasks, people and other records, answers your questions about them, and sends you reminders you ask for. This page explains what it keeps and what it does with it.

What we store

  • Your account: your Telegram user id or WhatsApp number, the display name your messaging app shares, your timezone and settings.
  • Everything you send: messages, and PDF files with the text read from them.
  • What ATYA makes from them: records such as tasks and people, its replies to you, and a log of every change, so you can see what was done and why.

We don't collect contacts, location, photos or anything else from your phone.

How it's used

  • To file your messages, answer your questions and send the reminders and briefs you set up. Nothing else.
    • {% if provider == "bedrock" %}
  • To do that, the text of your messages and files is processed by AI models hosted on Amazon Bedrock, inside the same Amazon Web Services account that stores your data.
    • {% else %}
  • To do that, the text of your messages and files is sent to Anthropic's Claude API for processing.
    • {% endif %}
  • Messages travel through Telegram or Meta (WhatsApp), under their own privacy policies.
  • Data is stored on Amazon Web Services servers in the United States.

We don't sell your data, show ads, share it with anyone other than the services above, or use it to train AI models. Each account's data is kept separate from every other account's by the database itself.

If you connect Google

  • ATYA reads, and never changes: your calendar events from yesterday to 30 days ahead, and the names and birthdays of your Google contacts.
  • It copies events into your records and turns upcoming birthdays into reminders, only for you. Your Google data is never sold, shared with anyone, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.
  • ATYA's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
  • Access tokens are stored encrypted. Disconnect any time on the Connect page, or remove ATYA's access in your Google account settings.

How long it's kept, and your control

  • Your data is kept until you delete it.
  • You can see and edit everything on the web view (send /web to the bot), and download all of it with /export or the Export button.
  • /delete permanently erases your account and all its data, including the log. Encrypted nightly backups are kept for 30 days, so deleted data is gone from them within 30 days.
