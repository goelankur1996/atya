A personal assistant that lives in your chats.

Tell ATYA what's going on. It helps you plan, keep track, remember and find things, and calls you when it matters.

Talk to it on WhatsApp or Telegram: a message, a voice note, a photo or a document. On the web, you see everything it keeps, and can fix or add to it.

Ask it to plan your week around your calendar, track your workouts or a project, remember what you promised Jim, find that note from March, wish your sister on her birthday, or research a good physio near you.

With their OK, it can also remind people in your circle: your dad about his evening walk, by message or call.

Soon: teach it your way. How it organises your things, researches for you, and what it asks and shows you. Then it starts doing things for you, not just keeping track.
