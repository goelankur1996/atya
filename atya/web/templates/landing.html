ATYA is a memory that lives in your chats. Tell it what's going on, in a message or a voice note, and it keeps track of the rest.

There's nothing new to learn: you use it on WhatsApp or Telegram, the way you'd message a friend. It files what you say into tasks, people and notes, and answers when you ask.

It can remember what you promised Rahul, remind you to call Jim at five, send you a short brief every morning, wish your sister on her birthday, or find a good physio near you.

Your records are yours alone. See and edit everything on the web, export it any time, or delete it with one command.
