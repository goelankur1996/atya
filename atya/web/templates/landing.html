ATYA is a personal assistant that lives in your chats. Tell it what's going on, and it helps you plan, keep track, remember and find things.

You talk to it on WhatsApp or Telegram, in a message or a voice note. On the web, you see everything it keeps, and fix or add to it.

It can plan your week around your calendar, track your workouts or a project, remember what you promised Rahul, find that note from March, wish your sister on her birthday, or research a good physio near you.

Soon you'll teach it your way: how it organises your things, how it researches for you, what it asks you and what it shows you. Then it starts doing things for you, not just keeping track.

Your records are yours alone. Export them any time, or delete everything with one command.
